Privacy Policy of STrack

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how STrack ("STrack", "we", "our", or "the App") handles information when you use our mobile application and related services.

STrack is designed to minimize the amount of personal information collected from users. The App does not access your device location, camera, photos, videos, contacts, microphone, or files.

By using STrack, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

STrack uses Supabase to provide account authentication and account management services.

When you create or use an account, information necessary for authentication may be processed, such as:

  • Email address
  • User account ID
  • Authentication information managed by Supabase

STrack does not request or collect additional personal information from your device unless you voluntarily provide information directly through the App.

1.2 Information You Voluntarily Provide

Depending on the features you use, STrack may store information that you voluntarily enter into the App.

STrack does not automatically collect information from your device for purposes unrelated to the features you choose to use.

1.3 Device Information

STrack does not intentionally collect or access sensitive device information such as:

  • Precise or approximate location
  • Contacts
  • Photos or videos
  • Camera data
  • Microphone or audio data
  • Files stored on your device
  • SMS or call history
  • Installed application information

1.4 Notifications

STrack may request permission to send notifications to your device. Notifications are used only for application-related messages, updates, reminders, or other relevant events provided by STrack.

Granting notification permission is optional. You can disable notifications at any time through your device settings.

2. Permissions Used by STrack

STrack does not require access to sensitive device features such as location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, videos, or device storage.

The App may request the following permission:

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS - allows STrack to send notifications to your device.

Apart from notification access, STrack does not request sensitive runtime permissions from your device.

3. How We Use Information

Information associated with your STrack account may be used only as necessary to:

  • Provide and operate the application
  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Authenticate users
  • Synchronize account-related data
  • Provide application functionality requested by the user
  • Send application notifications
  • Maintain application security and prevent abuse
  • Improve application reliability and functionality

STrack does not use personal information for unrelated advertising or profiling purposes.

4. Third-Party Services

4.1 Supabase

STrack uses Supabase for backend services, including authentication, database services, and account-related data management.

Information necessary to provide these services may be processed and stored by Supabase on behalf of STrack.

STrack does not use Supabase to obtain unrelated personal information from your device.

4.2 Notification Services

STrack may use platform notification services to deliver notifications to your device.

These services may process technical information necessary to deliver notifications, such as a notification or device token, depending on the notification technology used by the application and your device platform.

5. Sharing of Information

STrack does not sell your personal information to third parties.

Information may be processed by service providers only when necessary to operate the application, including authentication, database services, hosting, and notification delivery.

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law, regulation, legal process, or a valid governmental request.

We may further process or disclose information when reasonably necessary to protect the security, rights, and integrity of STrack, our users, or our services.

6. Data Storage and Security

Account-related information may be stored on secure infrastructure operated by STrack and its service providers, including Supabase.

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Account information is retained for as long as necessary to provide the STrack service or as required for legitimate legal, security, and operational purposes.

You may request deletion of your STrack account and associated personal information by using any account deletion functionality provided within the App or by contacting us using the email address below.

Certain information may be retained for a limited period when required by law, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, security, or other legitimate purposes.

8. User Choices and Permissions

STrack is designed to operate without requiring access to sensitive device permissions.

The only optional device permission requested by STrack is notification access.

You can enable or disable notifications at any time through your device settings.

9. Children's Privacy

STrack is not intended for children under the minimum age required by applicable law to independently use the service.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

10. Advertising

STrack does not display advertisements.

STrack does not use advertising services such as Google AdMob to display advertisements to users.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the application, our services, or applicable legal requirements.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
